Project story

Built to make provider discovery more focused

The confirmed concept progressively narrows a service request through Components 1, 2 and 4. Component 3 is an independent credibility contribution supporting the wider platform.

The research brings AI/ML-driven matching to Sri Lanka's maintenance service ecosystem through four coordinated research components.

Research topic

AI/ML-Driven Maintenance Service Provider Matching Platform for Sri Lanka

A focused academic investigation into relevance, context, credibility and trust-aware service-provider matching.
