Weda.lk academic research portfolio

Reliable service matching, shaped by context and trust.

An Integrated Hybrid Recommendation and Trust-Aware Review Intelligence Framework for Reliable Service Provider Matching

Weda.lk investigates how hybrid recommendation, temporal and contextual matching, and credible review intelligence can produce more relevant and explainable provider shortlists.

Explore ResearchView ComponentsResearch Documents
Recommendation journey20 → 10 → 5

A staged pipeline moves from broad relevance to contextual fit and finally a trust-aware top five. Component 3 remains an independent credibility contribution.

Research challenge

From a broad list to a relevant, trusted shortlist

Service discovery must balance initial relevance, time and context, and the quality of review evidence. The research separates those concerns into focused components.

Problem snapshot

Selection overload

Users need a clear route through a large provider pool. The precise formal research problem is awaiting an approved statement.

Solution overview

Progressive recommendation

Three sequential stages reduce candidates while an independent documentation contribution remains outside the ranking flow.

Research contributions

Four distinct components

Each contribution tackles a specific layer of reliable service-provider discovery.

Component 01

Hybrid Recommendation Engine

Analyzes natural-language service requirements and applies hybrid recommendation methods to identify 20 relevant providers.

Read more
Component 02

Temporal & Contextual Matching

Refines the 20 candidates using contextual and temporal criteria to select 10 suitable providers.

Read more
Component 03

Skill Verification & Credibility

An independent research contribution focused on provider skill verification and credibility; it is not a filtering stage.

Trust-Aware Review Intelligence

Evaluates review credibility and service-quality aspects to generate explainable rankings for the final top five.
