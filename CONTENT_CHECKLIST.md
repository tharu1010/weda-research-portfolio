# Weda.lk Content and Approval Checklist

Record owner, version, approval, source path, size and public/private status for every asset before publication.

## Research identity and content

- [ ] Official title, abstract, university, programme, group ID and approved email
- [ ] Literature survey, citations, existing approaches and research gap
- [ ] Formal problem, main/specific objectives and methodology
- [ ] Data sources, ethics, privacy, bias and retention details
- [ ] Technology stack, diagrams and approved application screenshots
- [ ] Evaluation plan, verified results, limitations and future work

## Assessments and files

- [ ] Proposal, PP1, PP2 and Final/Viva dates, weights and statuses
- [ ] Charter, proposal, checklists and progress reports
- [ ] C1/C2/C3/C4 and final reports
- [ ] Research papers and approved publication links
- [ ] Proposal, PP1, PP2 and final presentation files
- [ ] Ownership, copyright, confidentiality and supervisor approval recorded

## People and submission

- [ ] Four verified member names, roles, bios and approved photos/links
- [ ] Supervisor names and institutional affiliation
- [ ] Confirm GitHub Pages/course-web destination and JavaScript acceptance
- [ ] Owner review of every page
